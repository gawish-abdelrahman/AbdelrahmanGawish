I've run rooms with real stakes.

At the World Urban Forum (UN-Habitat, 2024), I trained and supervised a team of over 400 ushers and directed the Press Conference Hall, the venue for announcements from presidents, ambassadors, and senior UN officials, under strict international protocol.
